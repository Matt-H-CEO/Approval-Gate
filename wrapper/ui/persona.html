What's going on

The situation. A support assistant at Lumen Cloud (fictional) has drafted a reply to a customer. Someone has to decide whether it can be sent.

What could go wrong. A draft can read perfectly and still quote the wrong price, the wrong discount or the wrong refund window. Once it is sent, the customer has been promised it.

What HoldPoint does. It checks every figure in the draft against the price sheet and the policies, holds anything it cannot verify, and only lets the mock send run when the gate approves.
